📖 Understanding GT Analysis

PredT = Mean prediction when action IS happening (want HIGH, ideally >0.7)

PredF = Mean prediction when action is NOT happening (want LOW, ideally <0.2)

Gap = PredT - PredF (discrimination ability, want HIGH >0.3)

📈 Charts show epoch-by-epoch progress - green bars are PredT, red bars are PredF. Gap should widen over time.
