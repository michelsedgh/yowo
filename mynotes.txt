Let's Categorize ALL 157 Actions
Looking at the full class list, I can group them:

Instant/Transitional Actions (~1-3 seconds) - ~15% of classes
- Standing up (c154)
- Sitting down (c151)  
- Opening a door (c008)
- Closing a door (c006)
- Opening/Closing laptop (c047, c048)
- Turning on/off light (c104, c105)
- Opening/Closing refrigerator (c142, c143)
- Throwing X somewhere (c003, c024, c031, c036, c045, c058, c064, c074, c080, c101, c126)
- Grasping doorknob (c141)
- Sneezing (c153)
Short-Medium Actions (~3-8 seconds) - ~35% of classes
- Walking through doorway (c097)
- Taking X from somewhere (most "taking" actions)
- Putting X somewhere (most "putting" actions)
- Opening/Closing a bag, box, book (c021, c039, c041, etc.)
- Drinking from cup (c106)
- Taking medicine (c129)
- Awakening (c133, c146)
- Running (c150)
Sustained/Long Actions (~8-30+ seconds) - ~50% of classes
- Holding X (all "holding" actions - c000, c020, c026, c033, etc.)
- Working at table (c014)
- Playing with phone (c016)
- Talking on phone (c019)
- Watching/Reading book (c032)
- Working on laptop (c052)
- Sitting in/on chair (c059)
- Eating (c065, c156)
- Snuggling with blanket/pillow (c072, c078)
- Watching TV (c132)
- Lying on bed/sofa/floor (c122, c124, c134)
- Cooking (c147)
- Dressing/Undressing (c148, c155)
- Tidying actions (c004, c012, c037, etc.)
- Washing actions (c005, c013, c038, etc.)
